# Project SAFE — Sexual Awareness For Everyone

## About the Project

**Project SAFE (Sexual Awareness For Everyone)** is an educational website dedicated to increasing awareness about sexually transmitted diseases (STDs) and promoting healthy sexual practices.

The website provides accurate, reliable, and easy-to-understand information about STDs, prevention, testing, treatment, and available healthcare resources in Southern Leyte.

## Website Features

### 🏠 Home Page

* Welcome message
* Website objectives
* Quick navigation
* STD awareness banner

### 📚 About STDs

Provides information about:

* What STDs are
* Common symptoms
* Causes
* Risk factors
* Transmission methods
* Prevention strategies

### 📊 Southern Leyte Statistics

* HIV and STD case information
* Charts and graphs
* Annual reports
* Community awareness information
* Research findings

### 🦠 Types of STDs

The website provides information about:

* HIV/AIDS
* Gonorrhea
* Syphilis
* Chlamydia
* Genital Herpes
* Hepatitis B

### 🛡️ Prevention and Safety

Provides practical guidelines for reducing the risk of STD transmission, including:

* Practicing safe sex
* Regular testing
* Communicating with partners
* Seeking medical advice
* Getting vaccinated
* Avoiding needle sharing
* Avoiding unprotected sex
* Avoiding self-medication

### 🏥 Testing and Treatment Centers

Provides information about healthcare facilities in Southern Leyte where users can seek STD testing, treatment, and counseling.

Information includes:

* Hospital and clinic locations
* Contact information
* Operating hours
* Directions

### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

The FAQ section answers common questions such as:

* Is HIV curable?
* How often should I get tested?
* Can STDs be treated?
* What are the common symptoms?
* Can I have an STD without symptoms?
* Can condoms completely prevent STDs?
* Why is early treatment important?
* Where can I get tested in Southern Leyte?

### 📞 Contact and Help

Provides a contact section for users who need additional information or assistance.

> Personal names, phone numbers, and email addresses have been removed from this README.

## Project Objective

Project SAFE aims to:

* Increase awareness about STDs.
* Educate the public about prevention, testing, and treatment.
* Reduce stigma surrounding sexual health issues.
* Encourage regular health checkups and testing.
* Provide access to local healthcare resources in Southern Leyte.

## Awareness Message

> **"Knowledge is Protection: Learn, Prevent, Get Tested."**

Early detection, proper treatment, and preventive measures can help reduce complications and improve quality of life.

## Disclaimer

Project SAFE is an educational website and should not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Users with concerns about their sexual health should consult a qualified healthcare professional or visit an appropriate healthcare facility.
